About

Building better foundation models through better data.

I am currently a principal researcher on the HY Foundation Model Team at Tencent, where I lead the overall mid-training efforts for both vision-language models (VLMs) and large language models (LLMs), including CT, CPT, and long-context training. I have published more than 40 papers at leading conferences, including more than 20 papers as first or co-first author. My research focuses on how synthetic data can be used effectively to scale the capabilities of foundation models.

Previously, I contributed to the Qwen2.5 and Qwen3 model series. Graph-Reasoner is a collection of projects on graph reasoning; please feel free to explore our work.
