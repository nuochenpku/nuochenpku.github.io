I am currently a principal researcher on the HY Foundation Model Team at Tencent, where I lead mid-training efforts for the HY model series, including CT, CPT, and long-context training. I have published more than 40 papers at leading conferences, including more than 20 as first or co-first author. My research focuses on how synthetic data can be used effectively to scale the capabilities of large language models.

Previously, I contributed to the Qwen2.5 and Qwen3 model series. Graph-Reasoner is a collection of projects on graph reasoning; please feel free to explore our work.

Research & Hiring

We are looking for full-time researchers and self-motivated interns with strong hands-on skills and an interest in LLM training, data-centric AI, and large-scale experimentation.

Our current research interests include:

  • Improving the quality and diversity of synthetic data.
  • Understanding the optimal mix of synthetic and real data.
  • Developing effective scaling strategies for synthetic data.
  • Exploring when and to what extent synthetic data can replace real data.

If you are interested in data scaling laws, synthetic data generation, or mid-training strategies for LLMs, please feel free to contact me by email.
